Braids and diffeomorphisms (ICMU School 27 July - 5 August 2026)

In the summer of 2026 I gave a series of lectures on Braids and Diffeomorphisms for PhD students at the ICMU Summer School on Links, Braids and Diffeomorphisms of Surfaces. The guiding theme was the construction of quasi-morphisms on diffeomorphism groups of manifolds due to Gambaudo and Ghys, generalised and developed by Brandenbursky. I used it as a motivation to learn the basics on braid groups, quasi-morphisms and diffeomorphism groups of manifolds.

♥ I would like to thank the students for actively attending my lectures and extra classes, and for all questions and discussions (not only mathematical) during the school. I would also like to thank the students for their kind comments about the quality of my teaching. Of course, I thank all participants and staff for the fantastic atmosphere. ♥
