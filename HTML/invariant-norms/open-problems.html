Open Problems

    Autonomous norm.

  1. What is the diameter of the autonomous norm on ${\rm Ham}(\mathbf R^{2n})$?
    • It is known that the norm is bounded by 3 [Brandenbursky-Kędra arxiv:1603.02864], so the question is whether the diameter is 2 or 3.

  2. Is the diameter of the autonomous norm on ${\rm Ham}(M,\omega)$ infinite for any closed (or finite volume) symplectic manifold?
    • This is known to be true for closed surfaces and the 2-disc [Brandenbursky-Kędra-Shelukhin: arxiv:1207.0624, arxiv:1405.7931, arxiv:1602.03287].

    Burago-Ivanov-Polterovich [arxiv:0710.1412].

  3. Does there exist a group that does not admit a stably unbounded norm and yet admits a norm unbounded on some cyclic subgroup?

  4. Does there exist a finitely presented group G with finite abelianisation whose commutator length is unbounded but stably bounded?

  5. Does there exist an unbounded finitely presented group which admits no unbounded quasi-morphisms?
    • There exist a finitely generated group with these properties [Muranov arxiv:0909.2294].

  6. Does there exist a group G with finite abelianisation whose commutator length is stably bounded, but which admits a stably unbounded norm? In other words, does the existence of a stably unbounded norm on G yields existence of non-trivial quasi-morphisms? Is there a group G that admits no non-trivial quasi-morphisms but carries a norm that is unbounded on some cyclic subgroup. Solved:
    • The commutator subgroup [B∞, B∞] of the infinite braid group has trivial abelianisation, is stably unbounded and has commutator length bounded by 2 [Brandenbursky-Kędra arxiv:1402.3191].
    • The kernel of the Calabi homomorphisms $\mathsf{CAL}\colon{\rm Ham}^c(\mathbf R^{2n})\to \mathbf R$ is simple, has commutator length bounded by 2 and is stably unbounded [Kawasaki-2016].

  7. Is the fragmentation norm bounded for closed surfaces?

  8. Assume that n ≥ 3. Does the identity component of the group of volume preserving diffeomorphisms of the sphere $\mathbf S^n$ admit an unbounded conjugation-invariant norm? Does the identity component of the group of compactly supported volume preserving diffeomorphisms of the ball of finite volume admit an unbounded conjugation-invariant norm?

    Dichotomy.

  9. Find an example of a finitely presented group G and an element g ∈ G such that the sequence ∥gn∥ is unbounded and has sublinear growth.

    Diffeomorphisms.

  10. Let M be a smooth manifold. Is the group ${\rm Diff}^c_0(M)$ of compactly supported diffeomorphisms of M isotopic to the identity (uniformly) bounded?
    • The uniform boundedness is known to be true if either dimM ≠ 2, 4 or M has no handles in middle dimension: [Tsuboi-2008], [Tsuboi-2009]

    Hofer's norm.

  11. Is the diameter of the Hofer norm always infinite?
    • All known examples have infinite diameter.

  12. What are possible Hofer topologies on the infintie cyclic group?

    Lattices.

  13. Are lattices in semisimple Lie groups of higher rank bounded?
    • This is known to be true for S-arithmetic Chevalley groups and their subgroups of finite index [Gal-Kędra arxiv:1102.0423].
    • Cocompact lattices are not strongly bounded [Kędra-Libman-Martin].
