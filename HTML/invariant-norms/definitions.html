Definitions.

Notation:

  • The neutral element of any group is denoted by 1.
  • If G ⊴ H is a normal subgrup then the conjugation action of H on G is denoted by hgh−1 or hg or h(g); it is a left action.
  • H → Aut(G) - a left action of a group H on G; the action of h ∈ H on G is denoted by h(g).
