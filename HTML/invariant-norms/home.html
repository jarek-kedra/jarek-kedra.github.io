Invariant norms on groups

This website collects information about invariant norms on groups: definitions, examples, basic theorems, known results, applications, open problems and links to other resources.

The website has been written in markdown with some parts in pure html and compiled with pandoc. It also uses mathjax. It is maintained by me Jarek Kędra. Please make suggestions, point out mistakes, inform me about solved open problems etc.

The logo in the top left corner is a subgraph of the Cayley graph of the free group equipped with the conjuation invariant word norm associated with the standard generators.
