Scottish Mathematical Challenge

On June 10, 2026 I gave a talk On really big numbers for laureates of the Scottish Mathematical Challenge competition for school children. The aim of the talk was to introduce Graham's number.

The content of the talk:
  1. A brief history of writing numbers
    2. In this part, I ask the question how big numbers people needed in various historical times.
  2. Examples of numerical notation
    3. Babylon, Maya, China, Roman Empire, from Indian to Arabic.
  3. Modern times
    4. Again, the question is how big numbers we need today. In biology, physics and computing science the standard decimal positional notation plus using powers is sufficient. But not in Maths!
  4. Graham's number
    5. Graham came up with his number by solving a combinatorial problem. I discuss the problem, introduce Knuth's arrow notation and, finally, present Graham's number and discuss its properties.
