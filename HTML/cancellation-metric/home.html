Cancellation geometry of free groups

A reference website about cancellation length, bi-invariant metrics, and the large-scale geometry of free groups.

The cancellation length of a word is the fewest letters one must delete to leave a word representing the identity. Conjugates of a basis generator have length one, even when they are very long in the ordinary word metric. This simple observation leads to geometry quite different from the usual Cayley tree.

About this version

Unless stated otherwise, free groups have finite rank at least two and a specified free basis. Graph embeddings concern vertices with the graph distance. Definitions and statements are linked across pages; click Proof to expand an argument.

This is an AI-written working survey. Published results and public preprints are distinguished from AI-generated deductions and research drafts. “Lean verified: no” and “Human verified: no” mean that no corresponding verification has been documented. Bibliographic checks and computer tests do not constitute mathematical verification.

See the references and source register for precise citations and the editorial notes for remaining review tasks. Links to private source manuscripts require access to those files.

The design and the original Cayley-graph logo follow Jarek Kędra’s Invariant norms on groups website. The logo depicts a subgraph of the Cayley graph of \(F_2\) with its basis bi-invariant word metric.
