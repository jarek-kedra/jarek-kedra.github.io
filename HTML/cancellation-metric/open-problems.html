Open problems and questions

Questions raised in the survey, with links to the constructions that motivate them. A missing argument in a working draft is distinguished from a general open problem.

  • Coarse embedding into Hilbert space

    Does \((F_2,d_{\mathrm c})\) coarsely embed into a Hilbert space? The proposed embeddings of word spaces with bounded letter multiplicities concern particular subspaces and do not settle this question.

    Read the Hilbert-space discussion and its verification record.

  • Uniform bounds for changes of rank

    Can the quasi-isomorphisms between finite-rank nonabelian free groups be chosen with Lipschitz constants independent of both ranks? The construction currently included gives constants that may depend on the ranks.

    Read the flexibility statement and construction.

  • The structure of the outer quasi-automorphism group

    Is \(\operatorname{qOut}(F_n)\) simple? The subgroup constructions in the present draft do not resolve this question.

    Read the subgroup results.

  • Representatives and the ordinary word metric

    Does every class with a rational representative admit a representative that is also a quasi-isometry for the ordinary word metric? Failure of one representative to have this property is insufficient to answer the question.

    Read the distinction between representatives and outer classes.

  • Ultrafilters and scaling sequences

    The proposed rank-independence corollary compares cones formed using the same ultrafilter and the same scaling sequence. Whether the resulting cones are independent of these choices is not established in this survey.

    Read the precise corollary.

  • Unbounded word spaces in rank two

    The norm-preserving maps used for bounded word lengths need not be compatible as the length bound grows. A single isometric embedding of the full unbounded word graph into \(F_2\) is not supplied by that argument.

    Read the word-space embedding and its scope.
