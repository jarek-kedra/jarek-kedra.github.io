References

Published papers, public preprints, and the working manuscripts used for this survey are listed separately.

Bibliographical records were checked on 6 November 2026. “No arXiv version located” is recorded explicitly rather than replacing an absent preprint with an unrelated link. An arXiv DOI is not a journal DOI. Draft links below point to the supplied working documents and are not public publication records.

  • Published literature

    [BGKM] Michael Brandenbursky, Światosław R. Gal, Jarek Kędra and Michał Marcinkowski, The cancellation norm and the geometry of bi-invariant word metrics, Glasgow Mathematical Journal 58 (2016), no. 1, 153–176. Journal / DOI · arXiv:1310.2921 · corrected v3 HTML. The correction to Proposition 2.E appears in §6 of v3.

    [J] Henry Jaspars, The stable conjugation-invariant word norm is rational in free groups, Proceedings of the American Mathematical Society 152 (2024), no. 6, 2403–2412. Journal / DOI · arXiv:2304.03618. Main result: Theorem 1.1; commutator example: Examples 1.3(2) in v2.

    [KL1] Jarek Kędra and Assaf Libman, Are free groups of different ranks bi-invariantly quasi-isometric?, Glasgow Mathematical Journal 67 (2025), no. 3, 356–364. Journal / DOI · arXiv:2407.18027. Main rigidity theorem: Theorem 1.1.

    [DH] Andrew J. Duncan and James Howie, The genus problem for one-relator products of locally indicable groups, Mathematische Zeitschrift 208 (1991), 225–237. Journal / DOI. No arXiv version located. The input used here is the free-group power bound \(k\le2\operatorname{cl}(g^k)-1\) for \(g\ne1\) in the commutator subgroup; the stable form is \(\operatorname{scl}(g)\ge1/2\).

    [NR] Graham Niblo and Lawrence Reeves, Groups acting on CAT(0) cube complexes, Geometry & Topology 1 (1997), 1–7. Journal / DOI · arXiv:math/9702231. See §3 for the hyperplane description of the combinatorial metric.

    [C] Daryl Cooper, Automorphisms of free groups have finitely generated fixed point sets, Journal of Algebra 111 (1987), no. 2, 453–456. Journal / DOI. No arXiv version located. The bounded-cancellation lemma is on p. 454.

    [G] Edna K. Grossman, On the residual finiteness of certain mapping class groups, Journal of the London Mathematical Society (2) 9 (1974), 160–164. Journal / DOI. No arXiv version located. Used for Property A of free groups.

    [K] Jarek Kędra, On Lipschitz functions on groups equipped with conjugation-invariant norms, Colloquium Mathematicum 174 (2023), no. 1, 89–99. Journal / DOI · arXiv:2204.09373. Further reading on Lipschitz functions and partial quasimorphisms.

    [CZ] Danny Calegari and Dongping Zhuang, Stable W-length, in Topology and Geometry in Dimension Three: Triangulations, Invariants, and Geometric Structures, Contemporary Mathematics 560, American Mathematical Society, Providence, RI, 2011, pp. 145–169. Publisher / DOI · arXiv:1008.2219. §3.2, Proposition 3.3, gives the cone group construction for verbal metrics; its group argument applies to any bi-invariant metric. This is a published proceedings chapter, not a journal article.

    [KL2] Jarek Kędra and Assaf Libman, Directional asymptotic cones of groups equipped with bi-invariant metrics, Topology and its Applications 392 (2026), article 109924. Journal / DOI · arXiv:2207.12704. Journal issue dated 15 November 2026; bibliographic record already available at this survey’s cutoff. This directional construction should be distinguished from the ultrafilter cone in asymptotic cones.

  • Editorial notes

    This is a first survey version, not a claim of exhaustive coverage. The main draft proofs have been rewritten into the exposition, and substantial published arguments are explicitly labelled as outlines. The following points remain for editorial review:

    1. Confirm or update verification records for each AI-generated theorem; a “yes” should identify a verifier or a Lean file and checked version.
    2. Confirm whether “word complexes” means the word-incidence graphs defined here or a different complex and metric.
    3. Decide whether the intended Ulam convention is \(d_U\) or \(2d_U\); the obstruction in III.21 prevents silently suppressing that choice.
    4. Supply any newer rank-change manuscript if uniform constants independent of rank should be included. The retrieved draft establishes finite constants depending on the ranks.
    5. Expand the published proof outlines, especially the construction underlying the torsion theorem in cone torsion.
